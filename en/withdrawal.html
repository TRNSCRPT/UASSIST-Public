UAssist Transcript — Withdrawal

Last updated: November 2026 · Terms of Use · Privacy Policy · Nederlands

Did you top up credit and want the unused portion refunded? Within 14 days of a top-up payment, you can withdraw the unused portion without giving a reason.

1. When can you withdraw?

  • Topped-up credit: Within 14 days after payment, you can request a refund of the unused portion of a top-up through the same payment method.
  • Started transcriptions: Once you click Start transcript, the transcription is executed immediately and the right of withdrawal ends for that specific order. If a Cloud job fails due to a technical error, the untranscribed portion is automatically refunded to your credit.

2. Withdraw directly in the extension

  1. Open the UAssist Transcript extension and click Withdrawal at the bottom.
  2. Select the top-up (less than 14 days old), enter your name and optional email address, and click Confirm withdrawal.
  3. The unused amount is refunded automatically via Mollie to the same payment method.

3. Contact

Need help? Send a message via Contact / Report a problem at the bottom of the extension, email transcriptly.info@gmail.com, or open an issue on github.com/TRNSCRPT/UASSIST-Public.
